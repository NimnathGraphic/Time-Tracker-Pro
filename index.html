Help & Support Center

Welcome to TimeTrack Pro V4.00

TimeTrack Pro is a comprehensive staff time management system designed for Australian businesses with multiple shop locations. This system helps you track staff attendance, manage shops, and generate detailed reports.

New Features in V4.00

1. Staff Management Enhancements:

  • Edit staff details with individual edit buttons
  • Update staff information anytime

2. Accurate Work Hours Calculation:

  • Fixed work hours calculation algorithm
  • Accurate break time calculations
  • Correct weekend and holiday hour tracking

3. Advanced Pay Rates System:

  • General rates for Full-time, Part-time, Casual
  • Individual rates for specific staff members
  • Holiday rates with easy configuration
  • Choose from general rates or set custom rates

4. Dedicated Pay Rates Tab:

  • Centralized rate management
  • Easy rate editing and updating
  • Clear rate categorization

5. Auto-updating Holidays:

  • Holidays automatically update for current year
  • Easy holiday management for any year

6. Simplified Approval System:

  • No approval history clutter
  • Clean approval workflow

7. Multi-Currency Support:

  • Choose between AUD, USD, EUR, GBP
  • Currency symbol updates throughout system
